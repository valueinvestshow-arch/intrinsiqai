Questions

Before you sign up

Is this investment advice?

No. IntrinsiQAI is a research tool. It does the arithmetic on your assumptions and watches prices for you. It does not tell you what to buy or sell.

What does the AI actually do?

It reads new filings, summarises what changed, and compares the results with the assumptions you saved. It drafts starting cases for you to edit. It does not pick stocks or set your assumptions for you, and its summaries can be wrong, so the filing figures are always shown beside them.

Where does the data come from?

Financial statements come from the SEC's EDGAR database, which holds what each company itself filed. Prices come from a market-data provider and are shown with the time they were taken.

Which companies are covered?

Everything listed on the NYSE and Nasdaq, including foreign companies listed there. Funds and a few issuers that file no structured financial statements are searchable but have nothing to chart.

What does it cost?

$9.99 a month, or $90 a year. Both include everything. Joining the early-access list is free, and nothing is charged until you choose a plan after launch.

When does it launch?

The date is not fixed yet. People on the early-access list will be emailed the day it opens.

What happens to my email address?

It is used for one thing: telling you about IntrinsiQAI early access. It is stored in the EU, never sold, and you can unsubscribe or ask for deletion at any time. See the Privacy Policy.
