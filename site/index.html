Questions

Is this investment advice?

No. BuyBelow does the arithmetic on your assumptions and watches prices. It never tells you what to buy or sell.

Where does the data come from?

Financial statements come from the SEC's EDGAR database, as each company filed them. Prices will come from a licensed market-data provider.

What does the AI do?

It summarises new filings and compares them with the assumptions you saved. It does not pick stocks, and its summaries can be wrong, so the filed figures are always shown beside them.

When does it launch?

No date yet. Everyone on the waitlist gets one email the day it opens.

What happens to my email?

It is used only to tell you about BuyBelow. It is stored in the EU, never sold, and you can ask for it to be deleted. See the Privacy Policy.
